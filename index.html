§ I.

Prospectus

Offering of $RWA on the Solana network

Wall Street is tokenizing stocks, gold and treasuries. We tokenized a cow. The following clauses describe the Asset, what stands behind it, and what could go wrong. Please read them. The cow did not.

1. The Asset

1.1The Asset is one (1) cow, hereinafter "Bessie."

1.2Bessie is designated Really Weird Asset No. 001 and wears ear tag RWA-001.† She also wears a suit, for reasons of corporate governance.

1.3Holders of $RWA are referred to as "Shareholders." Selling is referred to as "liquidating the herd." Upward price movement, if it ever occurs, is referred to as "asset appreciation." These are jokes, not terms of a contract.

2. Backing

2.1$RWA is backed by nothing. This includes the cow.

2.2Bessie is a drawing. Shareholders acquire no claim on any milk, hide, horn, pasture or moo, now or later.

2.3$RWA is a memecoin on Solana, issued through pump.fun under pump.fun's standard launch terms.‡ Nothing in this document is an offer of a security.

3. Risk Factors

3.1The Asset is a cow. The Asset may moo.

3.2The Asset generates no yield. It generates methane.

3.3The price of $RWA can go to zero. The cow will not notice.

3.4Any Shareholder may liquidate the herd at any time. So may every other Shareholder, at the same time.

3.5The Company makes no promises. There is no roadmap to profit and no team that owes you anything.

3.6The Asset cannot read this prospectus and has not approved it.

† The tag is yellow. It is the only yellow thing the Company owns.

‡ The Company did not negotiate these terms. The cow was not consulted.
